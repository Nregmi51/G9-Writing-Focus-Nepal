Q5.6Recipe
Question

Write a recipe for making a cup of black tea in about 100 words. Use the clues given below.

Clues: Ingredients: water, tea leaves, sugar, ginger (optional) — boil water — add tea leaves and ginger — simmer for two minutes — add sugar — strain into cups — serve hot

Answer

Recipe for Black Tea

Ingredients: 2 cups of water, 2 teaspoons of tea leaves, sugar, a small piece of ginger

Method:

  1. First, boil the water in a pan.
  2. Add the tea leaves and crushed ginger to the boiling water.
  3. Let it simmer for two minutes.
  4. Then, add sugar as you like.
  5. Stir it well with a spoon.
  6. Strain the tea into the cups.
  7. Serve it hot.

Enjoy your hot black tea.

Q5.7Recipe
Question

Write a recipe for preparing vegetable momo in about 100 words. Use the clues given below.

Clues: Ingredients: flour, cabbage, carrot, onion, ginger-garlic paste, spices, oil, salt — knead dough — prepare the filling — roll the dough into small circles — fill and fold — steam for 10-12 minutes — serve with achar

Answer

Recipe for Vegetable Momo

Ingredients: flour, cabbage, carrot, onion, ginger-garlic paste, spices, oil, salt

Method:

  1. First, mix flour with water and knead it into soft dough.
  2. Cut the cabbage, carrot and onion into very small pieces.
  3. Mix them with ginger-garlic paste, spices, oil and salt. The filling is ready.
  4. Make small balls of the dough and roll them into thin circles.
  5. Put the filling in the middle and fold the edges.
  6. Steam the momos for 10 to 12 minutes.
  7. Serve them hot with achar.
Q5.8Recipe
Question

Write a recipe for making potato pickle (aloo achar) in about 100 words. Use the clues given below.

Clues: Ingredients: boiled potatoes, cucumber, sesame powder, chilli powder, lemon juice, salt, mustard oil, coriander — peel and cut — mix all — add oil and lemon juice — keep for 10 minutes — serve

Answer

Recipe for Aloo Achar

Ingredients: boiled potatoes, cucumber, sesame powder, chilli powder, lemon juice, salt, mustard oil, coriander

Method:

  1. First, peel the boiled potatoes and cut them into small pieces.
  2. Cut the cucumber into small pieces too.
  3. Put them in a big bowl.
  4. Add sesame powder, chilli powder and salt.
  5. Then, add mustard oil and lemon juice.
  6. Mix everything well with a spoon.
  7. Put chopped coriander on top.
  8. Keep it for ten minutes and then serve.

It tastes very good with rice.

Clues: a crow — very thirsty — finds a pitcher with little water — cannot reach — drops pebbles one by one — water rises — drinks and flies away

Answer

The Thirsty Crow

Once upon a time, a crow was very thirsty. He flew here and there to find water but he could not find any. At last, he saw a pitcher under a tree. There was a little water at the bottom. But his beak could not reach it. He thought for a while. Then he got an idea. He picked up small pebbles and dropped them into the pitcher one by one. Slowly, the water rose up. The crow drank the water and flew away happily.

Moral: Where there is a will, there is a way.

The Honest Woodcutter

There lived a poor woodcutter near a river. One day, his axe fell into the river while he was cutting a tree. He sat down and cried. Then the river goddess came out. She showed him a golden axe, but he said, "It is not mine." She showed him a silver axe, but he refused again. At last, she showed him an iron axe. He said happily, "This is mine." The goddess was pleased with his honesty. She gave him all three axes. A greedy neighbour heard this and tried the same trick. But he lost his own axe.

Moral: Honesty is the best policy.

Social media like Facebook, TikTok and Instagram are very popular among teenagers. I agree that they do more harm than good.

Climate Change and Its Effects on Nepal

By Our Correspondent

Climate change is one of the biggest problems of the world today. Nepal is a small country, but it is facing its serious effects.

The main cause of climate change is the increase in greenhouse gases. They come from burning fuel, cutting down forests and from factories. Because of this, the earth is becoming hot.

In Nepal, the effects can be seen everywhere. The snow on the Himalayas is melting fast. It creates the risk of glacial lake floods. Rainfall has become unpredictable. In some places there is too much rain, which causes floods and landslides. In other places there is drought, so farmers cannot grow crops. Diseases like dengue are also spreading in new places. The weather is getting hotter, and many animals and plants are in danger.

We can do many things to reduce the effects. We should plant more trees and not cut down forests. We should use public transport, solar energy and electric vehicles. We should also reduce waste and not burn plastic.

In conclusion, climate change is a problem for everyone. If we do not act now, our future generations will suffer. So, let us all work together to save our earth.

By Our Correspondent
